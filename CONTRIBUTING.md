# Contributing

**Not currently taking pull requests.** Issues and reports of real failures are genuinely welcome — see below. How skills here are authored is documented separately in [AUTHORING.md](./AUTHORING.md), for reference, not as an open invitation to submit new ones.

## Reporting a bug / suggesting a change

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/astutegrogan/claude-skills/issues). For a change to an existing skill, include what actually happened (the failure, not just the symptom) — that's what turns into the next rule. See [AUTHORING.md](./AUTHORING.md)'s bar for what makes a good rule; the same bar applies to a suggested one.
